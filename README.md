# 🎬 Netflix Dashboard

## 📖 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Netflix analytics dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into Netflix content distribution by analyzing movies and TV shows across different countries, genres, and release years. It helps users explore trends in Netflix content growth, content types, and genre popularity through dynamic visualizations and filters.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Streaming platforms such as Netflix contain thousands of titles, making it difficult to quickly understand content trends and distribution patterns.  

This dashboard aims to solve that problem by providing a visual summary of Netflix content data, helping users identify:
- 📈 The growth of Netflix content over time
- 🎥 The balance between Movies and TV Shows
- 🌍 Top contributing countries
- 🎭 Most popular genres
- 📊 Content distribution trends

---

## 📌 Key Metrics
The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

- 🎬 **Total Titles:** 7,966
- 🍿 **Total Movies:** 5,690
- 📺 **Total TV Shows:** 2,276
- 📊 **Percentage of Movies:** 71.43%

---

## ✨ Dashboard Features

### 🧮 KPI Cards
Displays:
- Total titles
- Total movies
- Total TV shows
- Percentage of movies

### 📈 Content Growth Line Chart
Shows how Netflix content has grown over time based on the year titles were added.

### 🌎 Top Countries Bar Chart
Highlights countries with the highest number of Netflix titles.

### 🍩 Titles by Type Donut Chart
Compares the distribution between Movies and TV Shows.

### 🎭 Top Genres Bar Chart
Displays the most common genres available on Netflix.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard by:
- 📅 Year
- 🌍 Country
- 🎬 Genre

---

## 💡 Key Insights
Some important insights discovered from the dashboard include:

- 🎥 Movies make up the majority of Netflix content, accounting for over 71% of all titles.
- 🇺🇸 The United States contributes the highest number of titles on Netflix.
- 🎭 Drama is the most dominant genre on the platform.
- 📈 Netflix content experienced significant growth between 2016 and 2020.
- 📺 TV Shows represent a smaller but steadily growing portion of the catalog.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations
- Some records contained missing or inconsistent values.
- 🧹 Data cleaning and transformation were required before visualization.
- 🌍 Certain titles belonged to multiple countries or genres, which may affect categorization accuracy.
- 🕒 The dataset may not reflect the most recent Netflix catalog updates.
